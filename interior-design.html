01 — Interior Architecture

Residential Redesign

925 Camino Lindo, Goleta, CA

AutoCAD, SketchUp, AI renders  ·  2026  ·  Independent  ·  Lauren Thompson

This project displays the redesign of a 6,098 sqft property, currently divided among eight residents, reimagined as a single-family home.

I lived in the “before” of this space. It was full of people I loved, but it was never designed to hold them well. This project reimagines the space, altering the floor plan and repurposing the rooms.

The project began with an AutoCAD floor plan. I measured every room and outdoor space on the property. Walls between two bathrooms and two bedrooms were merged to open up the space. I re-plotted the outdoor footprint to make room for a home gym, an outdoor patio set, and a pergola with an outdoor dining area. Next, I used SketchUp to bring the new design to life in 3D. Finally, I used NanoBanana AI to render every room photo realistically. The final product gives the viewer a clear vision of the refreshed home.
